Proposed Experiment 7

GraphRAG pipeline

Adds a seventh condition to the 6×3 ablation: the OSM knowledge graph is queried as a graph at inference — multi-hop neighbourhoods and the path connecting the two entities — instead of as isolated per-place attribute records.

Proposed addition to the unified 6×3 design — would make it 7×3. Cost: 3 domains × 3 strategies × 2 shot modes = 18 runs. Local (ego-network) GraphRAG only; global community-summary search is not used, as the task is per-row entity-pair classification rather than corpus-wide sensemaking.
