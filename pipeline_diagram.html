One pipeline, three spatial relation domains.

A unified 6-experiment ablation of where a knowledge graph helps a 20B LLM reason about space — injected at training, at input, at inference, or not at all — replicated identically over topological, cardinal and relative relations.

2 · Shared preparation (per domain)

OSM groundinggeocode every entity via Nominatim → offline osm_cache.json; eval rows selected to be fully geocodable
Level-balanced splitheld-out eval stratified per (label × ambiguity level L1–L5); remainder = train, zero overlap
Two training setsplain corpus→label CSV (no-KG) · prompt+OSM-evidence JSONL (OSM-KG)

3 · Models (GPT-OSS-20B, MXFP4 → bf16)

Base

no adapter — frozen pretrained weights

LoRA · no-KG

fine-tuned on the plain train split (~4M trainable params)

LoRA · OSM-KG

fine-tuned on OSM-evidence-augmented examples (KG seen only at training)

4 · The six experiments — where does the KG enter?

ExperimentModelKG @ trainingKG @ inputKG @ inferenceQuestion it answers
Exp 1 · Basebase———raw spatial ability of the LLM

Exp 3 · FT OSM-KGLoRA OSM-KG✓——does the KG internalize into weights?
Exp 4 · Base + KG@inputbase—✓—does static KG context help untouched weights?
Exp 5 · FT + KG@inputLoRA no-KG—✓—do fine-tuning and KG context stack?

✓ = OSM evidence enters at that point · KG@training = evidence embedded in the fine-tuning examples · KG@input = evidence prepended once to the prompt · KG@inference = evidence retrieved per reasoning step (RAG loop)
